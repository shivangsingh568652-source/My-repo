## Script

**Hook (0–2 sec):** Start with the problem or a specific question.  
**Context (2–8 sec):** Why might this matter? Keep it brief.  
**Demonstration (8–25 sec):** Show one step or example.  
**Takeaway (25–35 sec):** Restate the action in plain language.  
**Optional question:** Ask something specific and relevant to invite discussion.

## Production checks

- [ ] Vertical framing; important elements clear of interface overlays.
- [ ] Clear voice audio and captions checked against the recording.
- [ ] No unsupported guarantees or misleading before/after claims.
- [ ] Music, footage, and images are original or properly licensed.
- [ ] Title and description accurately represent the video.
- [ ] Export and upload settings checked against current YouTube Studio guidance.
