# Channel blueprint: Minute Momentum

A practical starting point for a YouTube Shorts channel. Adapt the audience, promise, name, and examples to your interests before launch.

## Brand foundation

**Working name:** Minute Momentum (availability not checked)  
**Niche:** practical productivity habits and simple planning techniques  
**Audience:** people who want realistic ways to get started, stay organized, and reduce everyday friction  
**Promise:** one small, testable idea in each video  
**Differentiator:** explain the idea plainly, show how to try it, and acknowledge when it may not suit everyone

### Channel description

> Small, practical ideas for making your day feel more manageable. Minute Momentum shares quick ways to plan, focus, and get started—one idea at a time. Try what works for you, skip what doesn’t, and tell me what you’d like to see next.

### Tagline options

- Small steps. More momentum.
- One useful idea at a time.
- Make the next step easier.

## Content pillars

1. **Getting started:** reduce friction around beginning a task.
2. **Planning:** lightweight ways to choose and sequence work.
3. **Focus:** practical environment and attention experiments.
4. **Reflection:** simple ways to notice what worked and adjust.

Keep advice framed as an experiment, not a universal rule or medical claim.

## Repeatable video structure

- **0–2 sec — Hook:** name a relatable moment or specific question.
- **2–8 sec — Context:** explain the common snag without a long intro.
- **8–25 sec — Demonstration:** show one practical step on screen.
- **25–35 sec — Takeaway:** summarize the action and invite a relevant comment.

Use a new opening line for each video. Avoid a long logo intro; the viewer should understand the topic immediately.

## Visual direction

- **Canvas:** vertical 9:16; keep text and faces away from the screen edges and interface overlays.
- **Look:** warm white background, deep navy text, and a bright lime accent; use high contrast.
- **Typography:** one bold sans-serif for key phrases and one readable sans-serif for captions.
- **On-screen text:** short phrases, large enough to read on a phone. Caption the spoken words and proofread them.
- **Audio:** clean voice recording; use original audio or audio cleared for your intended use.
- **Profile image:** simple high-contrast “MM” mark or a clear headshot.
- **Banner:** “Small steps. More momentum.” Check the current safe area and recommended dimensions in YouTube Studio before exporting.

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] The first seconds make the topic clear.
- [ ] There is one main takeaway, not several unrelated tips.
- [ ] The demonstration is understandable with sound off.
- [ ] Captions are accurate and readable.
- [ ] Any factual statement has been checked; add sources in the description if useful.
- [ ] Visuals and audio are original or appropriately licensed.
- [ ] Title accurately describes the Short; no misleading claims.
- [ ] Description includes context or sources where needed.
- [ ] Review the preview on a phone-sized screen before publishing.

## First-month learning goals

This first month is an experiment, not a guarantee of growth. Keep a simple log of topic, opening hook, length, and what viewers responded to. After 12 uploads, look for patterns in retention and comments; repeat promising formats and revise weak openings. Avoid drawing conclusions from a single video.

## Channel creation note

This blueprint does not create a YouTube account or reserve a name. Create the channel while signed in to Google, check name and handle availability, and review YouTube Studio’s current guidance and policies before publishing.
